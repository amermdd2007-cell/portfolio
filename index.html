About me. Student, designer and Flutter developer in Jordan.

I started with Figma on a tourism app I designed over eight months. Then I wanted my designs to be real, so I learned Flutter and shipped Bug Hunter myself: the design, the code, the store page, the poster and the videos that bring players.

I use AI tools, mostly Claude, to move faster on code and video. The ideas, the direction and every final decision are mine.
